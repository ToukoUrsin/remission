  - BCL2L1 amplification (dev-01, 03, 04, 07) → BCLi. Missing it at 1.5% (dev-01) regrew the tumour.

  - MAP2K1 K57N (dev-03) → MEKi. Dev-03 BCLi 2 alone left it alive.
